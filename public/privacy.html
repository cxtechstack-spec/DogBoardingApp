Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Dog Boarding App is a booking tool used by dog boarding businesses to manage reservations. This page explains what information the app handles and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Booking details: owner name, dog name, breed, care notes, vaccination records, dates of stay, and services selected.
  • Business settings: pricing, capacity, and staff notification details entered by the boarding business.
  • Google Calendar connection: if a business connects Google Calendar, we store an access token and the connected calendar's email address.

How we use Google data

When a business connects Google Calendar, the app uses the calendar.events permission only to create, update, and remove calendar events for its own bookings. We do not read, share, or sell any other calendar data.

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How information is shared

We do not sell personal information. Data is shared only with the services needed to run bookings: the business's HighLevel account (contacts, invoices, and notifications), payment processing through HighLevel, and Google Calendar when connected.

Security

Data is stored in a secured database. Access tokens are encrypted at rest.

Your choices

A business can disconnect Google Calendar at any time from the app's Settings page or from Google account permissions. To request access to or deletion of your information, contact us below.
